# Definition

Recognized revenue sums `amount` over rows booked to the fiscal year, computed by
[the revenue computation](/computations/revenue.md). This concept only narrates the
figure; the number itself is produced by a sanctioned, attestable computation, so a
consumer can confirm it was computed the blessed way rather than improvised.
