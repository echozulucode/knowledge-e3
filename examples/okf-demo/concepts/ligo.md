# LIGO

The Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory (LIGO) is a pair of
kilometre-scale interferometers in the United States. In 2015 it made the first
direct detection of [Gravitational Waves](/concepts/gravitational-waves.md), from
the merger of two black holes — a result recognised by the 2017 Nobel Prize in Physics.
