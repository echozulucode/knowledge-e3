# Gravitational Waves

Gravitational waves are ripples in the curvature of spacetime that propagate at the
speed of light. They are a direct prediction of [General Relativity](/concepts/general-relativity.md),
and were first observed directly by [LIGO](/concepts/ligo.md) on 14 September 2015.[^ligo-gw150914]

A passing wave alternately stretches and squeezes space along perpendicular axes — an
effect far smaller than the width of a proton over a kilometres-long detector.
