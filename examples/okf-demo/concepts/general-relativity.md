# General Relativity

General relativity, published by Albert Einstein in 1915, describes gravity not as a
force but as the curvature of spacetime caused by mass and energy. Among its
predictions are gravitational time dilation, the bending of light, and
[Gravitational Waves](/concepts/gravitational-waves.md).
